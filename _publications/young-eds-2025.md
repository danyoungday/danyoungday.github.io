This project is one of the starting points for [Project Resilience](https://project-resilience.github.io/platform/), a collaboration between the Cognizant AI Lab and the United Nations to use AI for Good. A web-app demo can be found at [https://landuse.evolution.ml/](https://landuse.evolution.ml/). The project was first presented at the United Nations AI For Good Summit 2023 in Geneva, then was brought to the NeurIPS 2023 Workshop: Tackling Climate Change with Machine Learning, where it received the *Best Pathway to Impact Award*. Then it was presented at Baylearn 2024 and finally found its home published in Environmental Data Science.
